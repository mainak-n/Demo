SIGMA: Unbranded Selection Intelligence

Machine Learning Gap Analysis Competitive Intel
Mission: Systematically identify and address unbranded selection gaps against emerging competitors (Temu, Shein) to ensure Amazon becomes the true "everything store."

The Challenge

Customers split shopping between Amazon and emerging competitors that offer extensive unbranded catalogs. Unlike branded items (which use UPCs), unbranded items lack consistent IDs, making traditional gap analysis impossible.

The Solution: Substitutability Analysis

Designed the SIGMA framework to find equivalent products using Text and Visual Matching.

Workflow Architecture:
  1. Scraping: Ingest competitor catalog data.
  2. Clustering: Apply FSM Text Matching & Image Recognition.
  3. Gap ID: Identify high-demand items missing from our catalog.
  4. Action: Feed leads to Seller Recruitment Dashboard.
