Generative AI Workflow Automation Problem Framing

Problem Statement

Approach & Solution

  • Engineering Leadership: Defined schemas and ingestion requirements, translating raw extracts into scalable production workflows.
  • Observability: Built dashboards (MTTR, uptime, false positives) and ran experiments to guide safe automation.
