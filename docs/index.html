Document Intelligence Platform

The Document Intelligence Platform is a professional research tool that uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to analyze PDF documents. It creates a local vector database of uploaded content to provide accurate, context-aware answers to natural language queries.

Note: For testing, upload a 3–5 page PDF. Since this is a free portal, larger or very long documents may slow down the system or cause it to crash.

CapabilityImplementation Detail
Data IngestionReads PDF files and only processes documents with more than 5 pages into the knowledge base.
Text ProcessingSplits content into overlapping chunks to preserve context relationships during retrieval.
