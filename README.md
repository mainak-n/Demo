## 🚀 Projects & Case Studies

The portfolio features three major strategic pillars:

### 1. [ASIN Productivity (Branded)](asin-productivity.html)
A strategic program optimizing the profitability and efficiency of Worldwide Head Brands by eliminating unproductive ASINs.
* **Focus:** Catalog Health, Profitability, Gap Analysis.
* **Impact:** Driving +1200 bps productivity improvement and $5B GMS impact.

### 2. [SIGMA (Unbranded Intelligence)](sigma.html)
A competitive intelligence engine utilizing Computer Vision and ML to identify and close selection gaps against emerging competitors (e.g., Temu, Shein).
* **Focus:** Machine Learning, Substitutability Analysis, Competitive Intel.
* **Impact:** Addressing an $18.7B GMS opportunity across NA and EU.

### 3. [Availability & GenAI Ops](availability-genai.html)
An operational workflow automation project leveraging Generative AI to fix availability defects at scale.
* **Focus:** GenAI Problem Framing, Signal Logic, Workflow Automation.
* **Impact:** Automating remediation for 95k+ incidents and contributing $75M in revenue.
